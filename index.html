Auction rules

1

Team Composition & Auction Purse

Six teams; 16 players per team including the preselected playing owner and strategist. Each team buys 14 players. Category quota: A+ 2 | A 2 | B+ 5 | B 5 | WD 2. Total purse 11,40,000, less owner 70,000 and strategist 85,000, leaves an auction purse of 9,85,000.

2

Objectives

Competitive Bidding: Enable owners and strategists to bid for preferred players with fair opportunities for all teams. Purse Discipline: Ensure each team completes its squad within its notional purse. The computerised system manages limits and calculations.

3

Auction Process & Bid Increments

All players will be offered through five computer-randomised batches (2026 batch sizes appear below). Opening bid: 5,000 for every player. Increments: 5,000 up to 50,000; 10,000 above 50,000 to 1,00,000; 20,000 thereafter. Teams may bid only in categories with vacancies.

4

Purse Protection

For each unfilled position, the system protects 70,000 for A+, A and B+, and 30,000 for B and WD. These are not minimum selling prices. When bidding for a player, protection for that position is released; all other vacant positions remain protected. The system determines each team’s maximum permissible bid.

Purse protection – maximum permissible bidA team has 2,00,000 remaining and needs one A+ (70,000 protected), one B (30,000) and one WD (30,000). For A+, it can bid up to 1,40,000 (retaining 60,000). For B or WD, it can bid up to 1,00,000 (retaining 1,00,000). Actual bids follow permitted increments.
After buying a playerA team starts with 9,85,000 available, 7,00,000 protected and 2,85,000 free. It buys a B player for 50,000. Available purse becomes 9,35,000; protection becomes 6,70,000; free capacity becomes 2,65,000. Only 20,000 of free capacity is used because the B position’s 30,000 protection is released.
5

Unsold Players & Recall

Every available player is offered once in the regular auction. Players without a successful bid enter the unsold pool and are offered again in the recall round, subject to vacancies and purse limits.

6

Automatic & Random Allocation

Sequence: Regular batches → recall of unsold players → final random allocation, only when every player has been offered and no further competitive bids are possible or forthcoming, except for the immediate allocation rule below.

A+, A, B+ (no surplus): When only one team has vacancies in a category, all remaining players in that category are allocated immediately at 70,000 each, including those scheduled in later batches. Otherwise, after recall and competitive bidding are exhausted, remaining players are randomly allocated among eligible teams at 70,000 each.

B and WD (surplus): After recall and bidding are exhausted, unfilled slots are completed by random selection and allocation from remaining eligible players at 30,000 each. Surplus players remain unselected. All allocations are mandatory, with no choice, rejection or exchange.

A+, A and B+ – two allocation situationsOne eligible team: Eleven of 12 A+ positions are filled; the sole team needing an A+ immediately receives the remaining player at 70,000, even if listed in a later batch.Multiple eligible teams: Two A players remain unsold and two teams each need one. After recall and competitive bidding end, each receives one by random draw at 70,000.
B and WD – surplus allocationSeveral teams: Three teams each need one B; eight B players remain. After recall, the system randomly selects and allocates three at 30,000 each; five remain unselected.One team: One WD vacancy and three WD players remain; after recall, one is randomly allocated at 30,000 without owner selection.
7

General Rules

The system enforces category quotas, bid increments, purse protection and deductions; no negative purse is permitted. A reconciliation break will verify vacancies, purses and unsold players before final completion. The Tournament Committee will address exceptions fairly.

8

Auctioneer’s call is final

The auctioneer runs the room; the screen records it. If the auctioneer’s call and the computer entry ever differ, the auctioneer’s call stands and the screen is corrected.
